When people say "I'm a Leo", they mean their Sun sign. It is one of three points that astrologers read first. Together they are often called the big three.

## The Sun: what you are here to be

The Sun sign describes the centre of a character: what a person is proud of, what they are trying to become, how they shine when things go well. You only need the day and month of birth to find it, because the Sun spends about a month in each sign.

## The Moon: what you need to feel safe

The Moon sign describes the private side: moods, habits, what comforts a person and what unsettles them. It is often the part of someone that only family sees. The Moon changes sign roughly every two and a half days, so you need the full date, and on some days the hour as well.

## The rising sign: how you meet the world

The rising sign, or ascendant, is the sign that was coming up over the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. It describes first impressions and the manner a person wears in public. It changes about every two hours and depends on where on Earth the birth took place, so you need the date, the hour and the place.

## How they work together

Think of a house. The rising sign is the front door, the Sun is the main room, and the Moon is the kitchen where the family actually lives. Two people with the same Sun sign can seem nothing alike because their Moon and rising signs differ.

## What you need to find each one

| Point | What you need |
|---|---|
| Sun | Day and month |
| Moon | Full date, sometimes the hour |
| Rising sign | Full date, hour and place |

If you do not know your birth hour, you still have a Sun, almost always a Moon, and all the planets. Only the rising sign and the houses are out of reach.
