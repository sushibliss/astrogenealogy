## Who is responsible

Ascendant is operated by **[legal name, address and contact email to be added]**. Write to that address about anything on this page.

## What we collect

- **Your account**: your email, your name and a protected form of your password.
- **Your family**: the names, dates and places you enter about yourself and your relatives, and anything you write in the notes.
- **The research**: the findings, documents and links the researcher adds to your request.
- **The free birth chart**: the date, hour and place you enter are used to draw the chart and are not stored.
- **The email list**, only if you join it: your email address and language.

## Why we use it

To carry out the research you asked for and show you the result. Where you have joined the email list, to send you what you signed up for. We rely on your request for the service, and on your consent for the email list and for the optional cookies described below.

## Who can see it

You, the researcher, and the people you invite with a private reader link. Invited readers see living relatives by relationship only, never by name, date or place. Nothing is public. Private pages are closed to search engines.

## What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, we do not match it against other people's trees, and we do not use it to search archives automatically.

## Living people

Please enter details about living relatives only with their knowledge. Their birth details are never shown to invited readers.

## Cookies

- **Necessary**: one cookie keeps you signed in, one remembers your light or dark theme and one remembers your cookie choice. These are always set.
- **Measurement and advertising**, only with your consent: if you accept, Google Analytics and the advertising tags of Google and Meta may be loaded on the public pages, to count visits and to learn which advertisements brought people here. They are never loaded on your requests, your tree, reader links or birth reports.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the foot of every page.

## How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you ask for deletion, your request, your tree and your account are removed. Email-list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, withdraw a consent you gave, and complain to your data protection authority. Write to the address at the top of this page.
