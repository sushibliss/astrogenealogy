## What exactly is Ascendant?

A private service that researches your family line by hand in archives and publishes the findings for you, together with a birth chart and a written reading for each ancestor whose full birth date is known.

## How is this different from a family tree website?

Tree websites match your data against millions of other trees and return suggestions in seconds. Ascendant does the opposite: one person reads the original records for your family, tells you what is documented and what is not, and never compares your data with anyone else's.

## How much does it cost?

It is free during the first version. When prices are introduced they will be published before you submit anything, and requests already submitted will be finished at no charge.

## How long does the research take?

It depends on the place and the period. Some lines can be traced in days, others need an archive to reply by post. You can see the status of your request at any time, and you are told plainly what is still open.

## Which countries can you research?

The research is strongest in Spain and Spanish-speaking countries. If your family comes from somewhere else, submit the request anyway and say so: you will be told before any work starts whether the records are within reach.

## I do not know my ancestors' birth times. Can they still have a chart?

Yes. Without a birth hour, a chart still shows the Sun and the planets by sign. It leaves out the rising sign and the houses, and it says when the Moon could be in either of two signs. Nothing is guessed.

## Do I need to believe in astrology?

No. The genealogy stands on its own, with sources. The birth charts are a way of meeting each ancestor as a person rather than a name and two dates. Read them in whatever spirit suits you.

## Who can see my family's information?

Only you, the researcher, and the relatives you invite with a private link. Invited readers never see details of living people. Nothing is public, and private pages are closed to search engines.

## Can I try it before starting?

Yes. You can cast a free birth chart without an account, and you can read a full sample report built from an invented family.

## Can I delete my data?

Yes. Write to us and your request, your tree and your account will be deleted.
